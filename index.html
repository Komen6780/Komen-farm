About Elkanah Farm

We’re a family-run farm in Nakuru dedicated to producing the freshest vegetables, eggs, and grains. Our commitment to organic and sustainable farming ensures you get nutrient-rich, chemical-free produce every season.

We’ve been featured in local markets and supply several restaurants and households daily.
